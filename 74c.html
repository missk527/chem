Potable Water

Key Notes

1) Potable Water
  • Potable water is water that is safe to drink.
  • Potable water is not the same as pure water.
  • Pure water contains only H₂O molecules.
2) Conditions for Potable Water
  • The level of dissolved substances must be low.
  • The pH should be between 6.5 and 8.5.
  • The water must not contain harmful microorganisms.
3) Fresh Water Sources
  • Fresh water contains low amounts of dissolved substances.
  • Rain is an important source of fresh water.
  • Fresh water may collect as surface water or groundwater.
4) Surface Water and Groundwater
  • Surface water includes lakes, rivers, and reservoirs.
  • Groundwater is stored underground in aquifers.
  • Aquifers are permeable rocks that trap water.
5) Treating Fresh Water
  • Large objects are removed using wire mesh.
  • Sand and gravel filter out smaller particles.
  • Water is sterilised to kill microbes.
6) Sterilisation Methods
  • Chlorine gas can be bubbled through the water.
  • Ozone may be used to kill microorganisms.
  • Ultraviolet light can also sterilise water.
7) Desalination
  • Desalination removes salt from seawater.
  • It is useful in places with little rainfall.
  • The process is expensive because it uses a lot of energy.
8) Distillation and Reverse Osmosis
  • Distillation boils seawater and condenses the vapor.
  • Reverse osmosis pushes water through a membrane.
  • The membrane allows water molecules through but blocks ions.
