13. Moles, Concentration and Volume

Key Notes (English)

Adapted from the provided speech. Focus: the relationship between moles, concentration and volume, plus how to solve simple and reacting-solution calculations.

1) Core Formula
  • The key equation links moles, concentration and volume.
  • You may see it written as moles = concentration × volume.
  • It is also commonly written as n = c × v, where n = moles, c = concentration and v = volume.
2) Correct Units
  • Volume must be measured in dm³ (cubic decimetres).
  • Concentration is measured in mol/dm³ or mol dm⁻³.
  • The slash in mol/dm³ means per.
3) Converting Volume
  • Many questions give volume in cm³, but the formula needs dm³.
  • To convert from cm³ to dm³, divide by 1000.
  • Example: 800 cm³ = 0.8 dm³.
4) Example: Finding Moles
  • If sodium hydroxide has a volume of 800 cm³ and a concentration of 0.2 mol/dm³, first convert the volume to 0.8 dm³.
  • Then use moles = concentration × volume.
  • 0.8 × 0.2 = 0.16, so the sample contains 0.16 moles.
5) Example: Finding Concentration
  • If hydrochloric acid has 1.5 dm³ of solution containing 0.6 moles, then concentration is:
  • concentration = moles ÷ volume
  • 0.6 ÷ 1.5 = 0.4, so the concentration is 0.4 mol/dm³.
6) Reacting Solutions: First Step
  • In reaction questions, start by writing down the known values clearly.
  • Example: potassium hydroxide has a volume of 30 cm³ and concentration of 0.5 mol/dm³.
  • Sulfuric acid has a volume of 25 cm³, but its concentration is unknown.
7) Reacting Solutions: Convert Units First
  • Convert all volumes into dm³ before using the formula.
  • 30 cm³ = 0.03 dm³
  • 25 cm³ = 0.025 dm³
8) Reacting Solutions: Calculate Known Moles
  • Find the moles of the substance you know first.
  • For potassium hydroxide: moles = 0.5 × 0.03 = 0.015
  • So there are 0.015 moles of KOH.
9) Use the Molar Ratio
  • Next, use the balanced chemical equation to find the molar ratio.
  • In the example, potassium hydroxide and sulfuric acid react in a 2:1 ratio.
  • This means 2 moles of KOH react with 1 mole of H₂SO₄.
10) Find the Unknown Moles and Concentration
  • If there are 0.015 moles of KOH, then the moles of sulfuric acid are 0.015 ÷ 2 = 0.0075.
  • Now calculate the unknown concentration using concentration = moles ÷ volume.
  • 0.0075 ÷ 0.025 = 0.3, so the sulfuric acid concentration is 0.3 mol/dm³.
