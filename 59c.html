Notes (Key points from the speech)

  • Condensation polymers are made from many monomers.
  • Polyesters are condensation polymers containing ester links.
  • They are formed from a dicarboxylic acid and a diol.
  • An –OH from the acid and an H from the diol form water.
  • An ester link joins the remaining atoms.
  • Each repeating unit forms two ester links and two water molecules.
  • n is used to represent large numbers of monomers.
  • Condensation polymers release a small molecule, usually water.
  • Monomers must have at least two functional groups.
  • Polyesters are biodegradable; addition polymers usually are not.
