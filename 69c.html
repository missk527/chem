Key Notes (English)

Topic: Pollution from Burning Fossil Fuels

1) Complete Combustion
  • Occurs when hydrocarbons burn with plenty of oxygen.
  • Produces carbon dioxide and water only.
2) Incomplete Combustion
  • Happens when there is limited oxygen.
  • Produces carbon monoxide and soot (particulates).
3) Particulates (Soot)
  • Solid carbon particles formed during incomplete combustion.
  • Can damage lungs when inhaled.
  • Cause global dimming by reflecting sunlight.
4) Carbon Monoxide
  • Colorless and odorless gas.
  • Binds to hemoglobin, reducing oxygen transport.
  • Can cause death in severe cases.
5) Sulfur Dioxide
  • Formed when sulfur impurities in fuels burn.
  • Can irritate lungs and contribute to acid rain.
6) Nitrogen Oxides
  • Formed at high temperatures from nitrogen and oxygen in air.
  • Common in car engines.
7) Acid Rain
  • Forms when sulfur dioxide and nitrogen oxides dissolve in clouds.
  • Produces sulfuric acid and nitric acid.
  • Damages buildings, metals, and plants.
8) Environmental Impact
  • Pollutants damage human health and ecosystems.
  • Acid rain makes soil more acidic and harms plant growth.
